## Maker comment thread starters

**"This is a brand, not a product. Why is it on PH?"**
> Honest answer: maybe it shouldn't be. It's the closer to a 4-launch sequence and PH's format doesn't quite fit a retrospective. Show HN is the canonical channel for this post. If you found the ecosystem useful, vote and share, but the link to follow is the Show HN comment thread, where the technical depth lives.

**"What's the business model behind the ecosystem?"**
> Lifetime memberships ($103, parent-brand level), per-product subscriptions, per-class pricing for the Academy, and recently a public REST API on FlashLearnAI with metered billing. The lifetime push is the high-margin anchor; everything else stacks on top.

**"How do you keep 9 products alive solo?"**
> The shared infrastructure. One auth, one database where it makes sense, one inbox, one brand. The cost of the 10th product is much smaller than the cost of the 1st. The constraint isn't engineering, it's attention. So the ecosystem is built around what one operator can hold in their head at once.
